The Account 1910

Tashjian Brothers, “Menk Bedk E Grvenk” (Columbia E661 / matrix 4973-1, 1910)

Background: The text is unusually explicit about collective loss, unheard grievance, and armed recovery. It directly contrasts passive lament with organized resistance. This is a strong conceptual fit for the project's collective grievance / defiance strand, but it is not necessarily a revenge narrative in the narrow sense.

Proposed role: Collective resistance / armed recovery / anti-subjugation
