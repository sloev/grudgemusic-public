Tashjian Brothers 1910 Columbia repertoire: Dalvorig and Grvetsek Dgherk

Background: Houshamadyan's account of the Capuchin college in Mezire (Mamuret-ul-Aziz/Harput region) says that after the 1908 Ottoman constitutional proclamation, the college orchestra played Armenian patriotic songs at public events, including “Pamp Voradan,” “Grvetsek Dgherk” and “Dalvorig.” This establishes that both titles circulated as patriotic repertoire in an Armenian institutional/public setting in the Harput region. It does not establish that the Tashjian recording derives from that exact local performance tradition, nor does it supply lyrics or authorship.
