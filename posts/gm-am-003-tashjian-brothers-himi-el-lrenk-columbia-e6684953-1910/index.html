The Account 1910

Tashjian Brothers, “Himi El Lrenk” (Columbia E668/4953, 1910)

Background: The Armenian Museum identifies “Himi El Lrenk” as one of the 28 songs recorded by the Tashjian Brothers in 1910 and reproduces the Columbia E668/4953 label. The museum does not publish a full transcription of this particular 1910 performance.

Proposed role: Collective protest / anti-subjugation / refusal of silence / armed resistance rhetoric

Why it fits / decision: This is an exceptionally strong song-family match for the project's core: collective grievance, accusation of oppression, forced displacement, chains, loss of arms and land, attacks on religious/cultural institutions, refusal of silence, and explicit call to resistance. It is not merely patriotic praise.
