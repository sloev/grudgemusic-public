ARSCH, ARSCH

Background: The artist's Bandcamp page identifies an 18-track ARSCH album released on 1 December 2020. It offers a limited cassette edition and digital album. The cassette uses professionally dubbed Type-I C-44 tapes, is hand-numbered, and has a different arrangement/mix from the digital edition. Tracks and durations are listed on the artist page: https://arsch.bandcamp.com/album/arsch

Where and when: Commune and barn studio, Bitsch, Valais, Switzerland; Date(s): Released Dec 2020 from earlier reels; Medium: Cassette / digital

Why it's here: Self-organised commune and barn-studio cassette/digital release; DIY communal life rather than a penal or medical institution.
