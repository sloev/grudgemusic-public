The Account 1911

Çakum Effendi, “Malandanzas del asker” (Gramophone 11-12734, 1911)

Background: The Hebrew University scholarly commentary identifies this as one of the Ladino songs about “askerlik” — military service imposed on Jews by Ottoman authorities from 1908. It notes that Çakum's version is close to Haim Effendi's earlier / parallel recorded version and that rare oral-tradition versions probably derive from these commercial recordings.
