Made Anyway CCGomecito / FM Aire Libre community radio, Rosario, Santa F

Tardes Nuestras recurring opening theme (working incipit “Dicen que no tenemos razón”; official title unknown)

Background: A 2017 Universidad Nacional de Rosario thesis records that the programme made contact with musicians and produced a song to serve as the cortina (opening/closing theme) of Tardes Nuestras. A 2020 La Capital article describes the recurring opening as a catchy rock song, quotes its opening lyric “Dicen que no tenemos razón,” and says that the song starts the programme. The station's official programme page links to its recorded-programme archive on RadioCut.

Where and when: CCGomecito / FM Aire Libre community radio, Rosario, Santa Fe, Argentina; Date(s): Programme created 2005; theme documented by 2017 and 2020; recording date unknown; Medium: Song embedded in recorded radio programmes; no standalone release identified; external musicians collaborated, resident authorship/performance unverified
