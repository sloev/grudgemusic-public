Papus Rache / Bar Kochba (Yiddish theatre repertory)

Background: A German-language discography compilation titled Zusammenstellung der Schallplatten mit Themen aus dem Orient lists a recording under the work heading Bar Kochba (Goldfaden):

Why it fits / decision: This classification is based on the work's explicit plot function and the named aria. It should not be upgraded to a historical example of Jewish-Roman retaliation or a record of collective grievance without analysis of the libretto. The wider operetta draws on the Bar Kokhba revolt and later Jewish national memory, but its plot is a nineteenth-century theatrical construction.
