Titta Ruffo, "Cortigiani, vil razza dannata" (Verdi, *Rigoletto*, Act II)

Recording: Gramophone matrix 2735f, 24 Nov 1908, with La Scala orchestra, cond. Carlo Sabajno (US issue Victor 92066); Ruffo also re-recorded the aria later

Background: Rigoletto turns on the courtiers who abducted his daughter: public denunciation before the revenge plan.

Why it's here: accusation step of the revenge sequence in Vol. 2.
