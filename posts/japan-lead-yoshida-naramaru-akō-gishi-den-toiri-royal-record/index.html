The Account

Japan lead: Yoshida Naramaru, *Akō Gishi-den Toiri* (Royal Record)

Background: Nichibunken’s Rōkyoku SP Record Digital Archive describes a collection of 9,998 SP records donated by collector Morikawa Tsukasa in 2014. Its public database includes label images for all digitized records, but sound is publicly available only when copyright protection has expired; otherwise audio is restricted to on-site access. The current archive overview reports 2,237 audio titles and 5,534 label-image titles (as of February 2023). The Toiri record's presence in the 2016 institutional prospectus establishes a Nichibunken holding, but its inclusion in the online searchable subset and its audio status still need item-level confirmation.

Why it fits / decision: The title points to the raid by the Akō retainers, the climactic collective act of vengeance in the Chūshingura narrative. This is a strong thematic lead for collective revenge / retaliatory justice in dramatic-historical storytelling, not evidence that the recorded performance recounts the historical raid accurately. The 47 retainers’ revenge story is a cultural narrative with a long and shifting performance history; do not collapse the fictionalized stage/folk narrative, later national memory, and the historical events into one evidentiary category.
