Made Anyway Trinidad · 1914

Jules Sims, "Native Trinidad Kalenda" I–II, 1914

Recording: Victor G-149/G-150, Port of Spain, 11 Sep 1914, with tamboo bamboo band

Background: kalenda are stick-fighting songs; tamboo bamboo bands played them on bamboo tubes after colonial authorities restricted skin drums (cite the ordinance before liner use).

Why it's here: music made with what the law left; fight songs.
