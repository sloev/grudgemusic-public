Candidate research lead: Vahan Boyajian, “Derzor Dasdaanaci” (Bilbil 2)

Background: Yıldız identifies “Derzor Dasdaanaci” as a popular song with many versions by different singers. She describes it as narrating the long and painful deportation of Armenians to Deir ez-Zor in Syria, and as a lament that symbolically depicts violence committed around 1915. She interprets the song as a site of memory and “postmemory,” carrying familial and cultural memory across generations and borders.
