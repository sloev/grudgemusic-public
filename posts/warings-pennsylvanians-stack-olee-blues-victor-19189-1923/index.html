The Account 1923

Waring's Pennsylvanians, “Stack O'Lee Blues” (Victor 19189, 1923)

Background: The Stagolee ballad family preserves a violent grievance narrative, but this specific 1923 Waring recording is described by the historical sources as an instrumental fox trot. It has no verified sung text on which to assess accusation, retaliation, punishment or spite. The historical incident itself is a killing after a personal dispute, not automatically a revenge narrative. Under the project's core–narrative–context distinction, this recording is at most a context/version witness that shows how a Black American folk narrative entered mainstream dance-band recording.
