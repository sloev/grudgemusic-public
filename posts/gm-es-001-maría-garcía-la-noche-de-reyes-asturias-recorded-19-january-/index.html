The Account 1939

María García, “La noche de Reyes” (Asturias; recorded 19 January 1939)

Background: The documented plot is a killing in response to an alleged sexual betrayal. Before classifying the work as revenge, distinguish: - the father's narrated motive; - the child's position as listener; - the ballad's narrative stance; - the performer's own stance, which is not established by the catalogue.

Proposed role: domestic violence, accusation and retaliatory killing in an Asturian ballad

Why it fits / decision: A strong, exact, publicly catalogued revenge-ballad recording, but not eligible for the pre-1926 core. Its value is as a post-cutoff comparison and as a pointer toward earlier Iberian ballad witnesses. No listening or transcription was performed in this pass.
