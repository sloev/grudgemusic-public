Made Anyway South Africa · 1988

SABC-barred records: Bernoldus Niemand "Snor City" (1988); Mzwakhe Mbuli "Change is Pain"

Background: SABC memos 273 (9 Feb 1989) and 322 (8 Nov 1990) barred them; censors scratched discs so they could not be aired.
