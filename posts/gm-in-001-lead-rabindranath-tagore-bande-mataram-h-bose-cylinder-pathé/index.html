The Account 1908

(lead) — Rabindranath Tagore, “Bande Mataram” (H. Bose cylinder / Pathé-H. Bose disc; 1890s–1908, date disputed)

Background: “Bande Mataram” became a major song of the Swadeshi movement and resistance to the 1905 partition of Bengal. Its circulation was entangled with political meetings, anti-colonial mobilisation, police searches and destruction/confiscation of nationalist recording material. The gramophone/cylinder was not simply a neutral imported technology: local entrepreneurs and activists used it to circulate political sound.

Proposed role: Collective grievance / anticolonial defiance / protest
