The Account 1910

Tashjian Brothers, “Akh Vasburagan” (Columbia E666/4974, 1910)

Background: The Armenian-language ERGIR page “ԱԽ ՎԱՍՊՈՒՐԱԿԱՆ” (“Akh Vaspurakan”) attributes the words to S. Jamkochyan. Its stanzas: - mourn Vaspurakan and the Armenian dead; - condemn captivity, foreign dependence and disunity; - call for Armenian unity and explicitly say “Պէտք է մենք լուծենք վրէժ հայկական” — approximately, “We must exact Armenian revenge”; - describe Van and its districts filled with bodies and fields stained with Armenian blood; - denounce the Ottoman army and artillery; - end with a call to take up rifles as Armenian communities and homes are attacked.

Performer: Tashjian Brothers — Mardiros, Nishan and Levon Der Sarkis.

Title: “Akh Vasburagan” (also transliterated “Akh Vaspurakan”).

Label / catalogue: Columbia E666/4974.

Session: New York, autumn 1910.

Release: 1911.

Paired side: “Zartir Vortiag” (Columbia E666/4975).

Language: Armenian.
