Out of Turn Oran region, Algeria · 1954

The grandmother of raï, banned from her own country's radio

Cheikha Rimitti could not read or write. She learned her trade singing at weddings and in the bars of western Algeria. In 1954 her song "Charrak Gattà" ("Tear, lacerate") went straight at the taboo of virginity and scandalised the pious.

After independence in 1962, the new government denounced her songs as "folklore perverted by colonialism" and banned her from radio and TV. She kept singing at weddings, then in Paris, until two days before her death in 2006.
