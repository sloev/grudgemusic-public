The Account 1918

Zabelle Panosian, “Khavarel Yem” (Columbia E4010, June 1918)

Background: The Canary Records title gloss is “I Am Darkened.” Armenian lexical sources confirm that խաւարել / խավարել can mean to darken, become gloomy, or be overwhelmed by sorrow, but this is only a lexical clue, not a translation of the song. The Panosian project includes translated song texts and historical notes, but the full lyric of this item has not yet been independently checked in this research pass.

Performer: Zabelle Panosian, soprano, with orchestra.

Title: “Khavarel Yem” (publisher's English gloss: “I Am Darkened”).

Original label / catalogue: Columbia E4010.

Matrix / take: 84458-1 is listed for “Khavarel Yem” in the Columbia E-Series discography. Canary Records' later digital reissue contains two performances labelled take 1 (2:49) and take 2 (3:18); do not assume take 2 is the same matrix or issue without …

Recording date/location: June 1918, New York, per Columbia E-Series discography and the Armenian Mirror-Spectator's reproduction of the original Columbia label.

Issue: Columbia E4010; the Mirror-Spectator identifies the original record and dates it June 1918.

Exact later access copy: Canary Records, I Am Servant of Your Voice: March 1917–June 1918, digital album. Bandcamp lists “Khavarel Yem (I Am Darkened) take 1” (track 8, 2:49) and take 2 (track 13, 3:18), with streaming and high-quality MP3/FLAC …
