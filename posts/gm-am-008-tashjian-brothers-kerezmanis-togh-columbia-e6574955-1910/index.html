The Account 1910

Tashjian Brothers, “Kerezmanis Togh” (Columbia E657/4955, 1910)

Background: Houshamadyan's Sassoun / Alahaydoyan collection preserves a song titled “Sasnou Kavar (Hrayr Tjokhki Hishadagin)” — “Land of Sassoun (In Memory of Hrayr Tzhokhk)”. The text praises Sassoun, names revolutionary figures, describes conflict with Ottoman forces, and includes a speaker threatening to destroy the Ottoman army. Houshamadyan attributes this particular text to an ARF-affiliated family from Shenig in the Moush Valley and cites the ARF Archives in Watertown, Massachusetts. This is valuable regional and community provenance, but it does not establish that these exact verses occur on Columbia E657/4955.

Performer: Tashjian Brothers — Mardiros, Nishan and Levon Der Sarkis.

Title on discography: “Kerezmanis Togh”.

Common/related title: “Hrayr Tzhokhki Yerke” / “Hrayr Tzhokhk’s Song”, according to the Armenian Museum.

Label and issue: Columbia E657/4955.

Session: New York, autumn 1910.

Release: 1911.

Language: Armenian.
